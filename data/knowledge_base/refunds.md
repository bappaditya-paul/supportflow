# Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Eligibility
* Customers are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of initial billing if services were inaccessible or misprovisioned.
* Duplicate charges verified in the billing ledger qualify for immediate automatic refund of the second charge.

## 2. Authorization Boundaries
* **AI Agents cannot issue, process, or promise refunds.**
* All refund requests must be formulated as a proposed recommendation and submitted to the human Support Lead for explicit sign-off.
* Once human approval is logged, the refund ticket is routed to Stripe/Finance for settlement.
